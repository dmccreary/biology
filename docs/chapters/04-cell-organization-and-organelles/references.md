# References: Cell Organization and Organelles

1. [Cell (biology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_(biology)) - Wikipedia - Foundational overview of cell theory and the structural differences between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells that this chapter's opening sections build on.

2. [Endomembrane system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endomembrane_system) - Wikipedia - Explains how the rough ER, Golgi apparatus, lysosomes, and vesicles work together as a protein-processing and trafficking pathway.

3. [Mitochondrion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitochondrion) - Wikipedia - Detailed structural and functional description of the mitochondrion, including the endosymbiotic evidence discussed in this chapter.

4. Molecular Biology of the Cell (7th ed.) - Bruce Alberts, Alexander Johnson, Julian Lewis, David Morgan, Martin Raff, Keith Roberts, Peter Walter - W.W. Norton - Alberts pioneered the comparative "Panel" figures that place every major organelle's structure and function side by side on one page, a reference-diagram format now standard for teaching organelle survey chapters.

5. Becker's World of the Cell (9th ed.) - Jeff Hardin, Gregory Bertoni (after Wayne Becker) - Pearson - Becker's textbook is credited with pioneering the extensive pairing of real electron micrographs with interpretive line drawings, training students to read authentic microscopy data alongside idealized organelle schematics.

6. [AP Biology: Cell Structure and Function](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/cell-structure-and-function) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on organelles, the endomembrane system, and cell size, organized to match the AP Biology unit sequence.

7. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free searchable full text with detailed chapters on internal cell organization, organelle structure, and the endosymbiotic origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts.

8. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on cell structure, including microscopy-based resources for visualizing organelles in living cells.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on cell structure, offering an alternative labeled-diagram treatment of prokaryotic and eukaryotic organelles.

10. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - College-level lecture notes covering cell biology fundamentals, including organelle function and the cytoskeleton.
