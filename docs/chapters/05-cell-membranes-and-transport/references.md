# References: Cell Membranes and Transport

1. [Cell membrane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_membrane) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive description of the fluid mosaic model, membrane protein types, and the selective permeability that governs transport.

2. [Osmosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmosis) - Wikipedia - Explains water movement across selectively permeable membranes and the water-potential concept used to predict cell behavior in different solutions.

3. [Active transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_transport) - Wikipedia - Covers primary and secondary active transport mechanisms, including the sodium-potassium pump cycle central to this chapter.

4. Human Physiology: An Integrated Approach (8th ed.) - Dee Unglaub Silverthorn - Pearson - Silverthorn pioneered the "Running Problem" case-based teaching device threaded through each chapter, and is widely credited for figures that connect molecular pump and channel mechanisms directly to whole-body homeostasis, making abstract transport proteins tangible.

5. Physiology (6th ed.) - Linda S. Costanzo - Elsevier - Costanzo's Board Review Series text is known for pioneering compact, analogy-driven explanations of membrane transport (comparing carrier proteins and pumps to everyday mechanical devices) that distill dense transport physiology into memorable, testable comparisons.

6. [AP Biology: Cell Structure and Function](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/cell-structure-and-function) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on membrane structure, diffusion, osmosis, and active transport aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapters on membrane structure and the transport proteins that regulate what crosses the plasma membrane.

8. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Interactive modules and short films on membrane transport and cell signaling, including animations of channel and pump mechanisms.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college textbook chapter on the plasma membrane and passive and active transport, with additional worked examples of tonicity problems.

10. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - College-level lecture notes covering membrane biology and transport mechanisms in the broader context of cell physiology.
