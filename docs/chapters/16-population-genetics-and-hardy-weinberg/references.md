# References: Population Genetics and Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium

1. [Hardy–Weinberg principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardy%E2%80%93Weinberg_principle) - Wikipedia - Derivation of the p² + 2pq + q² = 1 model, its five assumptions, and its use as a null hypothesis for detecting evolution in a population.

2. [Genetic drift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_drift) - Wikipedia - Explains random allele frequency change in small populations, including the founder effect and bottleneck effect covered in this chapter.

3. [Natural selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_selection) - Wikipedia - Overview of stabilizing, directional, and disruptive selection, with the population-level logic that distinguishes selection from drift.

4. Evolutionary Analysis (5th ed.) - Scott Freeman, Jon C. Herron - Pearson - Freeman and Herron pioneered the explicit "state the hypothesis, predict, test" narrative structure for teaching Hardy-Weinberg as a null-hypothesis model to be falsified with data, rather than a formula to memorize and plug numbers into.

5. Principles of Population Genetics (4th ed.) - Daniel L. Hartl, Andrew G. Clark - Sinauer - Hartl is credited with popularizing the worked-numerical-example pedagogy of plugging real allele-count data directly into the p and q equations, now the standard method for teaching Hardy-Weinberg calculations in introductory courses.

6. [AP Biology: Natural Selection](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/natural-selection) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on population genetics, Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, and evolutionary forces aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [Understanding Evolution](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/) - UC Museum of Paleontology, UC Berkeley - Teaching resource covering genetic drift, gene flow, and the mechanisms that shift allele frequencies in populations.

8. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on population genetics, including data-based activities on allele frequency change.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on population genetics with additional worked Hardy-Weinberg problems.

10. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - College-level lecture notes covering population genetics and the modern evolutionary synthesis.
