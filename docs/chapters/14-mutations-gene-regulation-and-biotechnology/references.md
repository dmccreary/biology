# References: Mutations, Gene Regulation, and Biotechnology

1. [Mutation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutation) - Wikipedia - Classifies silent, missense, nonsense, and frameshift mutations and explains the mutagens that increase mutation rates above the spontaneous background.

2. [Lac operon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lac_operon) - Wikipedia - Detailed description of inducible gene regulation in bacteria, including the roles of the repressor, operator, and catabolite repression.

3. [CRISPR gene editing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRISPR_gene_editing) - Wikipedia - Explains how guide RNA and the Cas9 endonuclease enable precise, programmable editing of the genome.

4. Molecular Biology of the Gene (8th ed.) - James D. Watson, Tania A. Baker, Stephen P. Bell, Alexander Gann, Michael Levine, Richard Losick - Pearson - Watson's text is credited with the original lac operon "on/off switch" circuit-diagram pedagogy, treating gene regulation explicitly as a logical circuit, an analogy borrowed since by countless introductory textbooks.

5. Introduction to Genetic Analysis (12th ed.) - Anthony J.F. Griffiths, Susan R. Wessler, Sean B. Carroll, John Doebley - W.H. Freeman - This text pioneered numbered, step-by-step recombinant-DNA and CRISPR workflow diagrams that walk students through a full biotechnology protocol as a sequence of decisions rather than an abstract list of laboratory tools.

6. [AP Biology: Gene Expression and Regulation](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/gene-expression-and-regulation) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on mutations, operons, epigenetics, and biotechnology tools aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [Learn.Genetics](https://learn.genetics.utah.edu/) - University of Utah - Interactive modules on gene regulation, epigenetics, and biotechnology, including animations of PCR, gel electrophoresis, and CRISPR.

8. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on gene regulation and biotechnology, including case studies on CRISPR and genetic engineering.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on gene expression, mutation, and biotechnology with additional worked examples of operon logic.

10. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapters on the control of gene expression, covering transcription factors, chromatin structure, and epigenetic regulation.
