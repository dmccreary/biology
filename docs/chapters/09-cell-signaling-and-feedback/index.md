# Cell Signaling and Feedback

## Summary

Cells do not act in isolation — they continuously send and receive chemical signals
that coordinate behavior across tissues and organisms. This chapter examines the three
stages of cell signaling (reception, transduction, response) through five major
receptor types, then traces how second messengers such as cAMP amplify signals through
phosphorylation cascades. Negative and positive feedback loops are analyzed as the
regulatory logic underlying homeostasis and developmental switches. The chapter
concludes with apoptosis — programmed cell death — as a signal-mediated response
essential for development and tumor suppression.
