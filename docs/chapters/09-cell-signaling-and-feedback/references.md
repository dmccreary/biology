# References: Cell Signaling and Feedback

1. [Cell signaling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_signaling) - Wikipedia - Overview of paracrine, autocrine, endocrine, and synaptic signaling and the reception-transduction-response framework used throughout this chapter.

2. [G protein-coupled receptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G_protein-coupled_receptor) - Wikipedia - Detailed explanation of the largest receptor family in the human genome, including the G-protein activation cycle and second-messenger amplification.

3. [Apoptosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apoptosis) - Wikipedia - Describes programmed cell death, the caspase cascade, and the intrinsic and extrinsic pathways covered in this chapter's closing section.

4. Molecular Biology of the Cell (7th ed.) - Bruce Alberts, Alexander Johnson, Julian Lewis, David Morgan, Martin Raff, Keith Roberts, Peter Walter - W.W. Norton - Alberts pioneered the modular, cassette-style signaling diagram that depicts each pathway as interchangeable input-relay-output blocks, a format that has become the standard visual grammar for teaching signal transduction.

5. Molecular Cell Biology (9th ed.) - Harvey Lodish, Arnold Berk, Chris Kaiser, et al. - W.H. Freeman - Lodish pioneered the tight integration of primary experimental evidence directly into signaling-pathway figures, showing students the actual data that established each step of a cascade rather than presenting the pathway as settled fact.

6. [AP Biology: Cell Communication and Cell Cycle](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/cell-communication-and-cell-cycle) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on signal transduction, second messengers, and feedback loops aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on cell signaling pathways and apoptosis, including case studies connecting signaling errors to disease.

8. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapter on cell signaling mechanisms, covering receptor types and intracellular signal cascades in molecular detail.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on cell communication with additional diagrams of GPCR and receptor tyrosine kinase pathways.

10. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - College-level lecture notes covering cell signaling and its role in development and disease.
