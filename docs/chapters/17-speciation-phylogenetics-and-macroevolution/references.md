# References: Speciation, Phylogenetics, and Macroevolution

1. [Speciation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speciation) - Wikipedia - Overview of allopatric and sympatric speciation, reproductive isolating mechanisms, and the biological species concept covered in this chapter.

2. [Cladistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cladistics) - Wikipedia - Explains how shared derived characters are used to construct cladograms and reconstruct evolutionary relationships.

3. [Adaptive radiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_radiation) - Wikipedia - Describes how a single ancestral species diversifies rapidly into many species, with Darwin's finches as a central example.

4. What Evolution Is - Ernst Mayr - Basic Books - Mayr originated the biological species concept itself and is credited with the clear, accessible prose style that made reproductive-isolation reasoning teachable to non-specialists, a model of clarity later textbooks explicitly emulated.

5. Phylogenetic Systematics - Willi Hennig - University of Illinois Press - Hennig is the founder of cladistics and originated the shared-derived-character (synapomorphy) reasoning method that every modern cladogram-building exercise in textbooks directly descends from.

6. [AP Biology: Natural Selection](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/natural-selection) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on speciation and phylogenetics aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [Understanding Evolution](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/) - UC Museum of Paleontology, UC Berkeley - Teaching resource with modules on speciation, phylogenetic trees, and how biologists read and construct cladograms.

8. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on speciation and phylogenetics, including a cladogram-building activity using real trait and molecular data.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on speciation and macroevolution with additional worked cladogram examples.

10. [Learn.Genetics](https://learn.genetics.utah.edu/) - University of Utah - Interactive modules connecting DNA sequence comparison to evolutionary relationships, relevant to the molecular clock concept in this chapter.
