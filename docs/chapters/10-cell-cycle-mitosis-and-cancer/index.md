# The Cell Cycle, Mitosis, and Cancer

## Summary

Every somatic cell division begins with the cell cycle — a precisely regulated
sequence of growth, DNA replication, and division. This chapter examines each phase
(G1, S, G2, and M) and the mechanics of mitosis from prophase through telophase,
followed by cytokinesis. Critical attention is given to the checkpoint machinery:
cyclin-CDK complexes that drive progression, and the tumor suppressor and
proto-oncogene proteins that prevent uncontrolled division. The chapter closes with
cancer biology — explaining how mutations in these regulatory genes convert normal
cells into malignant ones — and contact inhibition as a normal restraint on growth.
