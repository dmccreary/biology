# References: The Cell Cycle, Mitosis, and Cancer

1. [Cell cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_cycle) - Wikipedia - Overview of the G1, S, G2, and M phases, checkpoint controls, and the cyclin-CDK complexes that drive progression through the cycle.

2. [Mitosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitosis) - Wikipedia - Detailed description of prophase through telophase and cytokinesis, including the spindle apparatus and kinetochore attachment covered in this chapter.

3. [Tumor suppressor gene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumor_suppressor_gene) - Wikipedia - Explains how genes like Rb and p53 restrain cell division and how their loss, alongside oncogene activation, drives cancer.

4. Molecular Biology of the Cell (7th ed.) - Bruce Alberts, Alexander Johnson, Julian Lewis, David Morgan, Martin Raff, Keith Roberts, Peter Walter - W.W. Norton - Alberts pioneered pairing the cyclin-CDK oscillation graph directly with a circular cell-cycle clock diagram, a combination that made the abstract logic of checkpoint regulation visually intuitive and is now widely copied.

5. The Biology of Cancer (3rd ed.) - Robert A. Weinberg - Garland Science - Weinberg originated the "hallmarks of cancer" framework itself as a teaching device, organizing an otherwise overwhelming body of cancer biology into a memorable, testable checklist that nearly every biology course now uses to structure cancer instruction.

6. [AP Biology: Cell Communication and Cell Cycle](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/cell-communication-and-cell-cycle) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on the cell cycle, mitosis, and cancer aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on cell division and cancer biology, including case studies on checkpoint failure and tumor suppressor loss.

8. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapter on the cell cycle and its regulation, covering cyclins, CDKs, and checkpoint mechanisms in molecular detail.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on the cell cycle with additional diagrams of mitotic stages and checkpoint regulation.

10. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - College-level lecture notes covering the cell cycle, cancer biology, and the genetic basis of tumorigenesis.
