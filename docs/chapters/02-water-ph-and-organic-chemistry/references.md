# References: Water, pH, and Organic Chemistry

1. [Properties of water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Properties_of_water) - Wikipedia - Detailed treatment of water's polarity, hydrogen bonding, cohesion, adhesion, and thermal properties that make aqueous biochemistry possible.

2. [PH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PH) - Wikipedia - Explains the logarithmic pH scale, its derivation from hydrogen ion concentration, and its role in chemistry and biology.

3. [Buffer solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffer_solution) - Wikipedia - Covers how conjugate acid-base pairs resist pH change, including the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation used to describe blood and cytoplasmic buffering.

4. Biochemistry (9th ed.) - Lubert Stryer, Jeremy Berg, John Tymoczko - W.H. Freeman - Stryer pioneered the exceptionally clear, color-coded molecular "cartoon" diagram style for depicting hydrogen bonding and functional groups, a visual convention that became the standard for biochemistry textbooks that followed.

5. Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry (8th ed.) - David Nelson, Michael Cox (after Albert Lehninger) - W.H. Freeman - Lehninger's original text pioneered the explicit, step-numbered proton-transfer arrow diagrams for acid-base and buffer chemistry, making the mechanism of buffering visually traceable rather than purely equation-based.

6. [AP Biology: Chemistry of Life](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/chemistry-of-life) - Khan Academy - Free lessons on water's properties, pH, and buffers with practice problems aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college textbook chapter on water, acids, bases, and buffers, offering a second worked explanation of the concepts covered in this chapter.

8. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Searchable library of short films and interactive modules, including resources on molecular structure and the chemistry underlying cellular processes.

9. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - College-level lecture notes and problem sets covering biochemistry fundamentals, including functional groups and macromolecule assembly reactions.

10. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free searchable full text of the standard cell biology reference, with chapters on the chemical components of cells.
