# References: Non-Mendelian Inheritance and Chromosomal Genetics

1. [Dominance (genetics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominance_(genetics)) - Wikipedia - Explains complete dominance, incomplete dominance, and codominance, providing the conceptual foundation for the ABO blood type and snapdragon examples in this chapter.

2. [X-linked inheritance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-linked_inheritance) - Wikipedia - Describes how genes on the X chromosome are inherited differently in males and females, including the hemizygosity that makes X-linked recessive traits more common in males.

3. [Down syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_syndrome) - Wikipedia - Overview of trisomy 21 as a case study in nondisjunction and aneuploidy, connecting the molecular error to its clinical features.

4. Genetics: A Conceptual Approach (7th ed.) - Benjamin A. Pierce - W.H. Freeman - Pierce pioneered the side-by-side ratio-comparison table (9:3:3:1 versus 9:3:4 versus 9:7) that makes modified dihybrid ratios instantly diagnosable, a device this chapter's epistasis section directly echoes.

5. Human Genetics: Concepts and Applications (13th ed.) - Ricki Lewis - McGraw Hill - Lewis pioneered the case-study-driven approach to teaching pedigree analysis, opening chapters with real patient and family stories that ground abstract inheritance patterns in human medical genetics rather than hypothetical organisms.

6. [AP Biology: Heredity](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/heredity) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on non-Mendelian inheritance, sex linkage, and chromosomal abnormalities aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [Learn.Genetics](https://learn.genetics.utah.edu/) - University of Utah - Interactive modules on inheritance patterns, including multiple alleles, sex-linked traits, and chromosomal disorders.

8. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on inheritance and genetic disorders, including pedigree-based case studies.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on non-Mendelian inheritance with additional worked examples of linkage and recombination frequency.

10. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapters on chromosome structure and genetics, useful background for the chromosomal abnormalities covered in this chapter.
