# References: Scientific Foundations and Atomic Chemistry

1. [Scientific method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_method) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of the iterative cycle of observation, hypothesis, experimentation, and revision that underlies all of biology's investigative practice.

2. [Chi-squared test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chi-squared_test) - Wikipedia - Explains the statistical logic of comparing observed to expected counts, including degrees of freedom and critical values used throughout genetics problem sets.

3. [Chemical bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_bond) - Wikipedia - Surveys ionic, covalent, hydrogen, and van der Waals interactions with diagrams of electron sharing and transfer relevant to every biological molecule.

4. The Basic Practice of Statistics (9th ed.) - David S. Moore, William Notz - W.H. Freeman - Moore pioneered the "data analysis before probability theory" sequence adopted across statistics education (the GAISE framework), teaching students to explore real data and graphs before formal inference — the same order this chapter uses for standard deviation and chi-square.

5. Chemistry: The Central Science (14th ed.) - Theodore L. Brown, H. Eugene LeMay - Pearson - Brown pioneered the macroscopic-to-molecular-to-symbolic three-level teaching framework for atomic structure and bonding, now a standard convention for connecting the invisible behavior of electrons to observable chemical properties.

6. [AP Biology: Chemistry of Life](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/chemistry-of-life) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on atomic structure, bonding, and the elements of life, organized to match the AP Biology unit sequence.

7. [Understanding Science](https://undsci.berkeley.edu/) - UC Berkeley Museum of Paleontology - An interactive flowchart and set of teaching modules that reframe the scientific method as a nonlinear, iterative process rather than a fixed checklist of steps.

8. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free, peer-reviewed college biology textbook with chapters on the chemical foundations of life, offering an alternative worked explanation of atomic structure and bonding.

9. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - Full syllabus, lecture notes, and problem sets from MIT's introductory biology course, useful for extending the chemistry-of-life foundations to a college level.

10. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Free, scientifically vetted videos, virtual labs, and classroom activities spanning the biology curriculum, searchable by topic including chemistry and data analysis.
