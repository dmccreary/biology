# References: Evidence for Evolution and Mechanisms of Change

1. [Evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution) - Wikipedia - Broad overview of evolutionary theory, including the mechanisms of natural selection, genetic drift, and gene flow covered throughout this unit.

2. [Charles Darwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Darwin) - Wikipedia - Biography of Darwin, his voyage on HMS Beagle, and the observations that led to On the Origin of Species.

3. [Endosymbiotic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbiogenesis) - Wikipedia - Describes the evidence for the bacterial origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts, including the double membrane, circular DNA, and 70S ribosomes discussed in this chapter.

4. Evolution (4th ed.) - Douglas J. Futuyma, Mark Kirkpatrick - Sinauer - Futuyma's text is widely credited as the field-defining pedagogical synthesis that established the now-standard structure of presenting converging, independent lines of evidence — fossil, anatomical, molecular, and biogeographic — as a unified case for common descent.

5. The Selfish Gene (40th anniversary ed.) - Richard Dawkins - Oxford University Press - Dawkins pioneered the "gene's-eye view" analogy for natural selection, reframing evolution from the replicator's perspective in a way that became one of the most influential conceptual teaching devices in the history of evolutionary biology education.

6. [AP Biology: Natural Selection](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/natural-selection) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on evidence for evolution and mechanisms of natural selection aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [Understanding Evolution](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/) - UC Museum of Paleontology, UC Berkeley - Comprehensive, peer-reviewed teaching resource covering the history of evolutionary thought, the evidence for evolution, and common student misconceptions.

8. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on evolutionary evidence, including case studies on comparative anatomy and molecular phylogenetics.

9. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on the evidence for evolution with additional worked examples comparing homologous and analogous structures.

10. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapter on the evolution of the eukaryotic cell, covering the endosymbiotic origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts.
