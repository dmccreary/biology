# References: Thermodynamics and Enzyme Kinetics

1. [Enzyme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enzyme) - Wikipedia - Explains how enzymes lower activation energy, the induced fit model, cofactors, and the major modes of enzyme regulation and inhibition.

2. [Gibbs free energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibbs_free_energy) - Wikipedia - Derivation and interpretation of the free energy equation used throughout this chapter to determine reaction spontaneity.

3. [Adenosine triphosphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adenosine_triphosphate) - Wikipedia - Structural and functional description of ATP as the cell's energy currency, including the thermodynamics of its hydrolysis.

4. Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry (8th ed.) - David Nelson, Michael Cox (after Albert Lehninger) - W.H. Freeman - Lehninger's text pioneered the enzyme kinetics teaching sequence built around the Michaelis-Menten and Lineweaver-Burk plots, a progression that has shaped how generations of students develop intuition for Km and Vmax.

5. Physical Biology of the Cell (2nd ed.) - Rob Phillips, Jane Kondev, Julie Theriot, Hernan Garcia - Garland Science - Phillips and colleagues pioneered an explicitly quantitative, order-of-magnitude estimation approach to teaching bioenergetics, encouraging students to reason numerically about free energy and reaction rates rather than treat thermodynamics as purely qualitative.

6. [AP Biology: Cellular Energetics](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/cellular-energetics) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on enzymes, free energy, and coupled reactions aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapters on how cells obtain energy and the catalytic mechanisms enzymes use to accelerate reactions.

8. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on metabolism, covering thermodynamics, free energy, and enzyme regulation with additional worked examples.

9. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Interactive modules and short films illustrating enzyme structure, active sites, and the effects of temperature and pH on catalysis.

10. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - College-level lecture notes and problem sets covering enzyme kinetics and metabolic regulation.
