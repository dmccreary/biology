# References: Photosynthesis

1. [Photosynthesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photosynthesis) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle, including the overall equation and historical discovery timeline.

2. [Calvin cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_cycle) - Wikipedia - Step-by-step description of carbon fixation, reduction, and RuBP regeneration, including RuBisCO's dual carboxylase/oxygenase activity.

3. [C4 carbon fixation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C4_carbon_fixation) - Wikipedia - Explains the spatial separation of CO2 capture and the Calvin cycle in C4 plants, including Kranz anatomy and its adaptive advantage in hot climates.

4. Campbell Biology (12th ed.) - Lisa Urry, Michael Cain, Steven Wasserman, Peter Minorsky, Jane Reece (originated by Neil Campbell) - Pearson - Campbell Biology popularized the widely copied "Z-scheme" diagram mapping electron energy against reaction sequence for the light reactions, a visualization convention now standard across nearly every biology textbook that teaches photosynthesis.

5. Plant Physiology and Development (6th ed.) - Lincoln Taiz, Eduardo Zeiger, Ian Max Møller, Angus Murphy - Sinauer - Taiz and Zeiger pioneered the integrated biochemical-physiological comparison table for C3, C4, and CAM photosynthesis that ties each pathway's biochemistry directly to the climate it evolved to suit, a format widely adapted by introductory textbooks.

6. [AP Biology: Cellular Energetics](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/cellular-energetics) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and practice problems on the light reactions, the Calvin cycle, and C3/C4/CAM strategies aligned to the AP Biology course framework.

7. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Short films and interactive modules on photosynthesis and plant adaptation, including data-based activities on light capture and carbon fixation.

8. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook chapter on photosynthesis with additional worked diagrams of the light reactions and Calvin cycle.

9. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapter on energy conversion in chloroplasts and mitochondria, useful for comparing the two organelles' electron transport chains.

10. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - College-level lecture notes covering cellular energetics, including photosynthesis and its relationship to respiration.
