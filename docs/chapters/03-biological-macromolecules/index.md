# Biological Macromolecules

## Summary

All living matter is built from four classes of biological macromolecules, and this
chapter provides a thorough examination of each. Beginning with carbohydrates as the
primary energy currency and structural material, the chapter moves through lipids
(including phospholipids as membrane components), proteins (from amino acid sequence
to quaternary structure and denaturation), and nucleic acids (DNA and RNA structure).
Understanding macromolecular structure-function relationships is the conceptual
foundation for Cell Biology, Cellular Energetics, and Molecular Biology.
