# References: Biological Macromolecules

1. [Carbohydrate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbohydrate) - Wikipedia - Overview of monosaccharide, disaccharide, and polysaccharide structure, including the glycosidic bond chemistry that distinguishes starch, glycogen, and cellulose.

2. [Lipid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipid) - Wikipedia - Surveys fatty acids, triglycerides, phospholipids, and sterols, explaining the amphipathic structure that underlies membrane self-assembly.

3. [Protein structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_structure) - Wikipedia - Detailed explanation of primary, secondary, tertiary, and quaternary structure with diagrams of the forces that stabilize each level.

4. Molecular Biology of the Cell (7th ed.) - Bruce Alberts, Alexander Johnson, Julian Lewis, David Morgan, Martin Raff, Keith Roberts, Peter Walter - W.W. Norton - Alberts pioneered the schematic, simplified molecular-cartoon convention for depicting proteins, nucleic acids, and macromolecular complexes, an approach so widely copied it now defines how textbooks visualize structure-function relationships.

5. Campbell Biology (12th ed.) - Lisa Urry, Michael Cain, Steven Wasserman, Peter Minorsky, Jane Reece (originated by Neil Campbell) - Pearson - Neil Campbell pioneered the "Figure Walkthrough" and inquiry-based figure caption, prompting students to predict a macromolecule's function from its structure before revealing the answer, an interactive-reading device widely adopted by later texts.

6. [AP Biology: Chemistry of Life](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-biology/chemistry-of-life) - Khan Academy - Video lessons and practice sets on carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, and nucleic acids matched to the AP Biology unit on macromolecules.

7. [Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/) - National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf) - Free full-text chapters on the chemical components and macromolecular structures of cells, including detailed protein-folding discussion.

8. [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) - OpenStax - Free college biology textbook with a dedicated chapter on biological macromolecules, offering worked examples of condensation and hydrolysis reactions.

9. [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) - Howard Hughes Medical Institute - Interactive modules and short films on protein structure and DNA, including 3D structure viewers useful for visualizing macromolecular folding.

10. [7.013 Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - Lecture materials and problem sets covering biochemistry and macromolecular structure at the college level.
