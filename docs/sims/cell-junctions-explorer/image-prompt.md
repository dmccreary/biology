## Technical Specifications

- Aspect ratio: landscape 4:3

## Overall Layout

The image is divided into four equal quadrants (2x2 grid), each showing a cross-sectional view of two adjacent cells with a different type of cell junction between them. A thin white dividing line separates the four panels.

- **Upper-left quadrant:** Tight Junction (animal cells)
- **Upper-right quadrant:** Desmosome (animal cells)
- **Lower-left quadrant:** Gap Junction (animal cells)
- **Lower-right quadrant:** Plasmodesma (plant cells)

Each quadrant shows two adjacent cells with their plasma membranes clearly visible and the junction structure at the interface between them.

## Color and Structure Specifications

### All Quadrants — Common Elements

- **Plasma membrane** — thin orange/gold (#F39C12) line forming the boundary of each cell, clearly visible in all four panels
- **Intercellular space** — narrow pale gray (#BDC3C7) gap between the two cells
- **Cell cytoplasm** — pale warm beige (#F5F0E8) fill inside each cell
- Each cell should occupy roughly half the quadrant width, meeting in the center

### Upper-Left: Tight Junction

- **Two rectangular epithelial cells** side by side with clear apical (top) and basal (bottom) orientation
- **Tight junction seal** — bright red (#E74C3C) band running horizontally across the intercellular space near the apical surface, shown as multiple interlocking protein strands
- **Claudin/occludin proteins** — dark red (#C0392B) paired transmembrane stalks that cross from one cell's membrane to the other, forming the interlocking seal. Show 4-5 pairs of these interlocking proteins
- The intercellular space should be visibly sealed (closed) above the tight junction and visibly open below it
- Must have at least 40 px of clear space at the junction site for a callout marker

### Upper-Right: Desmosome

- **Two adjacent cells** with a disc-shaped junction between them
- **Dense cytoplasmic plaques** — thick purple (#8E44AD) rectangular bars on the inner surface of each cell's membrane at the junction site
- **Cadherin proteins (desmoglein/desmocollin)** — medium purple (#9B59B6) paired stalk proteins spanning the intercellular space, connecting the two plaques. Show 5-6 pairs
- **Intermediate filaments** — dark purple (#7D3C98) thick wavy lines looping through each plaque and extending deep into the cytoplasm of each cell. Show bundles of 3-4 filaments on each side
- The desmosome should look like a symmetric disc or button connecting the two cells

### Lower-Left: Gap Junction

- **Two adjacent cells** with a cluster of cylindrical channels between them
- **Connexon hemichannels** — blue (#2980B9) cylindrical structures, each spanning one cell's membrane. Show 3-4 complete gap junction channels (each = two connexons docked end-to-end)
- **Connexin subunits** — show one connexon in cross-section view (ring of six dark blue (#2471A3) subunits around a central pore) alongside the longitudinal view
- **Small passing molecules** — light blue (#5DADE2) small circles/dots shown passing through the channels from one cell to the other, representing ions and second messengers
- The gap between the two cells should be very narrow (~2-4 nm implied) at the junction site

### Lower-Right: Plasmodesma

- **Two adjacent plant cells** with thick cellulose cell walls
- **Cell walls** — thick bright green (#2ECC71) layers on the outer surface of each cell, much thicker than the plasma membrane
- **Plasmodesma channel** — a narrow passage through the cell wall, lined by continuous plasma membrane (orange), connecting the two cells' cytoplasm
- **Desmotubule** — dark green (#1E8449) thin strand running through the center of the plasmodesma channel, representing compressed ER
- **Annular space** — narrow pale region between the desmotubule and the outer membrane lining, where molecules pass
- Show 2-3 plasmodesmata channels through the wall to indicate they occur in clusters
