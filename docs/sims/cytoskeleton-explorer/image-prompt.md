## Subject

A comparative illustration showing the three major cytoskeletal filament types side by side. The image is divided into three vertical panels (left, center, right) each showing one filament type. Each panel shows both a longitudinal (side) view and a cross-section view of the filament, with protein subunits clearly visible. Motor proteins and associated structures are included where relevant.

## Layout — Three Vertical Panels

### Left Panel (~33%): Microfilaments (Actin Filaments)

- **Actin filament (longitudinal)** — Two intertwined helical strands of spherical G-actin monomers forming an F-actin double helix; color: #5DADE2 (light blue). Individual spherical monomers clearly visible along both strands. The helix should be oriented vertically with the plus end at the top and the minus end at the bottom. At least 12-14 monomers visible per strand.

- **G-actin monomer** — Individual spherical subunit clearly distinguishable within the helix; color: #3498DB (medium blue). Each monomer should be a distinct sphere, ~20 px diameter.

- **Plus end** — The faster-growing end of the filament at the top; slightly more spread/open appearance; color: #2ECC71 (green tint). Visually distinct from the minus end.

- **Minus end** — The slower-growing end at the bottom; slightly more compact; color: #E74C3C (red tint). Visually distinct from the plus end.

- **Cross-section inset** — Small circular cross-section view in the lower-left corner of this panel showing the two-strand arrangement viewed end-on; color: #5DADE2. Diameter ~7 nm implied by the thin profile.

### Center Panel (~33%): Microtubules

- **Microtubule (longitudinal)** — Hollow cylindrical tube made of 13 protofilaments, each composed of alternating alpha-tubulin and beta-tubulin heterodimers stacked vertically; oriented vertically with plus end at top, minus end at bottom. The hollow lumen should be visible. Color: alpha-tubulin #F39C12 (orange), beta-tubulin #2C3E80 (dark blue).

- **Alpha-tubulin subunit** — Spherical orange subunit in the heterodimer pair; color: #F39C12. Alternates with beta-tubulin along each protofilament.

- **Beta-tubulin subunit** — Spherical dark blue subunit in the heterodimer pair; color: #2C3E80. Alternates with alpha-tubulin.

- **Cross-section inset** — Circular cross-section in the lower portion showing the ring of 13 protofilaments surrounding a hollow center (lumen); color: alternating #F39C12 and #2C3E80. The hollow center should be clearly visible as white/empty space. Diameter ~25 nm implied.

- **Kinesin motor protein** — Small two-headed walking figure on the surface of the microtubule, positioned near the top (plus end), with two globular heads and a coiled tail carrying a small cargo vesicle; color: #27AE60 (green).

- **Dynein motor protein** — Similar two-headed walking figure on the surface near the bottom (minus end), walking in the opposite direction from kinesin, also carrying cargo; color: #8E44AD (purple).

### Right Panel (~33%): Intermediate Filaments

- **Intermediate filament (longitudinal)** — Rope-like twisted bundle of fibrous protein strands; multiple coiled-coil dimers wound together into a thick rope structure; oriented vertically; color: #E67E22 (warm orange). No distinct plus/minus ends (non-polar).

- **Coiled-coil dimer** — Two alpha-helical protein chains wound around each other, visible at the top where the rope partially unravels to show substructure; color: #D35400 (darker orange).

- **Tetramer** — Four coiled-coil strands forming a staggered assembly, visible in a partially unwound section; color: #E67E22 and #D35400 alternating.

- **Cross-section inset** — Circular cross-section showing the dense, solid core (no hollow lumen, unlike microtubules); color: #E67E22. Diameter ~10 nm implied.

- **Keratin filaments** — A small cluster of intermediate filaments shown at the bottom-right, suggesting the dense network found in epithelial cells; color: #F0B27A (light orange).
