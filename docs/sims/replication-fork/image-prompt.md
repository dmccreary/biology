## Overview

The image shows a Y-shaped replication fork with the parental double-stranded DNA on the left unwinding into two daughter strands on the right. All key enzymes and structural features must be clearly visible as distinct, color-coded shapes.

## Spatial Layout

The fork opens from **left to right**:

- **Left side (left quarter):** Intact double-stranded parental DNA helix, shown as two intertwined strands (medium gray #888888 and light gray #AAAAAA) with visible base-pair rungs. A small round protein (topoisomerase, gold #DAA520) sits ahead of the fork on the unreplicated DNA.
- **Center (fork junction):** A large ring-shaped enzyme (helicase, bright blue #2196F3) sits at the junction point where the two strands separate. The helicase is wedge-shaped or donut-shaped, clearly splitting the two strands apart.
- **Upper right (leading strand, top half):** The template strand runs 3'→5' from the fork toward the upper right. A large oval enzyme (DNA Polymerase III, forest green #2E7D32) sits on this strand, synthesizing a new complementary strand (bright green #4CAF50) continuously. A short red segment (RNA primer, red #F44336) is visible at the very start of the new strand near the fork. Small oval proteins (SSB proteins, teal #009688) coat the single-stranded template between helicase and Pol III.
- **Lower right (lagging strand, bottom half):** The template strand runs 5'→3' from the fork toward the lower right. Multiple short segments of new DNA (Okazaki fragments, royal blue #1565C0) are visible, each preceded by a short red RNA primer segment (#F44336). A DNA Pol III enzyme (forest green #2E7D32, same color as leading strand Pol III) sits on the most recently started fragment nearest the fork. A smaller oval enzyme (primase, orange #FF9800) is shown laying down a new primer ahead of the newest fragment. Further from the fork, a medium-sized enzyme (DNA Polymerase I, purple #7B1FA2) is shown removing a primer from an older fragment. Between two completed fragments, a small enzyme (DNA Ligase, hot pink #E91E63) is shown sealing the nick where fragments join.

## Structure Details (each must have clear landing zone ≥30 px)

- **Parental DNA double helix** — two intertwined strands in medium gray (#888888) and light gray (#AAAAAA) with horizontal base-pair rungs between them; occupies the left quarter of the image. Must show clear helical twist.

- **Topoisomerase** — small, round, gold (#DAA520) protein sitting on the parental DNA ahead of (left of) the fork junction. Appears to grip both strands of the helix.

- **Helicase** — large, ring-shaped or wedge-shaped enzyme in bright blue (#2196F3) at the fork junction. Clearly positioned at the point where the two parental strands separate. This is the most prominent enzyme at the fork center.

- **Single-strand binding proteins (SSB)** — multiple small oval/bean shapes in teal (#009688) coating the single-stranded regions of both template strands between the helicase and the polymerases. Show at least 4-5 SSB proteins clustered along each exposed strand.

- **Leading strand template** — the upper template strand running from the fork toward upper right, in medium gray (#888888). Single-stranded where exposed.

- **New leading strand** — continuous new strand in bright green (#4CAF50) complementary to the leading template, synthesized from the fork rightward. Runs parallel to the template.

- **DNA Polymerase III (leading)** — large oval enzyme in forest green (#2E7D32) sitting at the growing tip of the new leading strand (upper right area). Clearly larger than SSB proteins.

- **RNA primer (leading strand)** — a short red (#F44336) segment at the 5' start of the new leading strand, near the fork origin. Clearly different color from the green new strand.

- **Lagging strand template** — the lower template strand running from the fork toward lower right, in light gray (#AAAAAA). Single-stranded where exposed.

- **Okazaki fragments** — 3-4 short segments of new DNA in royal blue (#1565C0) on the lagging strand, each about 1/6 the length of the leading strand. They are synthesized in the direction AWAY from the fork (toward the left).

- **RNA primers (lagging strand)** — short red (#F44336) segments at the 5' end of each Okazaki fragment. At least 3 visible primers.

- **Primase** — small oval enzyme in orange (#FF9800) on the lagging template strand near the fork, shown laying down a new RNA primer. Distinct from the larger Pol III.

- **DNA Polymerase III (lagging)** — large oval enzyme in forest green (#2E7D32) on the lagging strand, extending the most recent Okazaki fragment nearest the fork.

- **DNA Polymerase I** — medium oval enzyme in purple (#7B1FA2) on the lagging strand further from the fork, positioned where it is removing/replacing an RNA primer with DNA.

- **DNA Ligase** — small enzyme in hot pink (#E91E63) positioned at a nick between two completed Okazaki fragments, shown sealing the gap.
