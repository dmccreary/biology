# Mitosis Stage Explorer — Image Generation Prompt

**No text, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, leader lines, or annotation marks anywhere in the image.**

## Overview

Create a biological textbook illustration showing the five stages of mitosis in a single horizontal sequence from left to right: Prophase → Metaphase → Anaphase → Telophase → Cytokinesis. Each stage occupies approximately one-fifth of the image width, separated by thin vertical light gray divider lines.

## Technical Specifications

- **Aspect ratio:** landscape 4:3
- **Resolution:** 1200 × 900 px
- **Style:** biological textbook illustration, clean line art with light color fills, white background, AP Biology high school level
- **No text, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, leader lines, or annotation marks anywhere in the image.**

## Layout

Five panels arranged left to right, each showing one cell in the appropriate mitotic stage. Each cell is drawn as a large rounded shape (~180 px wide) centered in its panel with adequate margin above and below for marker placement.

## Stage-by-Stage Structure Details

### Panel 1 — Prophase (leftmost ~20%)

- **Cell outline:** large oval, light gray border, pale cream fill
- **Chromosomes:** 4 condensing X-shaped sister chromatids visible inside the cell; drawn as thick rods in two colors — 2 blue chromosomes and 2 red chromosomes (representing two homologous pairs). Chromosomes are randomly distributed within the cell.
- **Centrosomes:** two small orange circles at opposite sides of the cell, with thin green lines (spindle fibers) beginning to radiate outward from each
- **Nuclear envelope:** dark gray dashed circle around the chromosomes, partially fragmented (gaps visible on left and right sides) to show it is breaking down
- **Nucleolus:** small dark purple circle inside the nuclear envelope area, fading/partially transparent to show it is disappearing

### Panel 2 — Metaphase (left-center ~20%)

- **Cell outline:** large oval, light gray border, pale cream fill
- **Chromosomes:** 4 fully condensed X-shaped sister chromatids perfectly aligned in a single vertical line at the center of the cell (the metaphase plate). Same blue/red color coding as Prophase.
- **Metaphase plate:** shown as the vertical alignment zone — a subtle dashed magenta vertical line at center
- **Spindle apparatus:** thin green lines (kinetochore microtubules) extending from two orange centrosomes at left and right poles to the kinetochore (center) of each chromosome
- **No nuclear envelope** — completely gone

### Panel 3 — Anaphase (center ~20%)

- **Cell outline:** elongated oval (wider than other panels), light gray border, pale cream fill — the cell is visibly stretching horizontally
- **Chromosomes:** 8 individual chromatids (4 moving left, 4 moving right) pulled toward opposite poles. Each chromatid is V-shaped with the centromere leading. Left set: 2 blue + 2 red. Right set: 2 blue + 2 red.
- **Spindle fibers:** green lines from each centrosome (orange circles at poles) attached to the V-point of each chromatid, visibly shortened compared to metaphase
- **Polar microtubules:** additional green lines overlapping at the cell center, pushing the poles apart

### Panel 4 — Telophase (right-center ~20%)

- **Cell outline:** elongated pinched shape — a cleavage furrow is forming at the center (waist narrowing)
- **Chromosomes:** at each pole, chromosomes are decondensing — drawn as looser, more diffuse blobs rather than distinct X or V shapes. Still blue/red colored but less defined.
- **Nuclear envelopes:** two new dark gray dashed circles reforming around each cluster of chromosomes at the two poles
- **Cleavage furrow:** visible indentation at the center of the cell, with a ring of small pink dots representing the actin-myosin contractile ring
- **Spindle remnants:** faint green lines, mostly disappeared

### Panel 5 — Cytokinesis (rightmost ~20%)

- **Two daughter cells:** completely separated into two round cells side by side, each with a light gray border and pale cream fill
- **Nuclei:** each cell has a complete dark gray circle (nuclear envelope) containing decondensed, diffuse chromatin (very loose blue/red coloring)
- **Contractile ring remnant:** small pink pinch mark between the two cells where they just separated
- **Each cell is visibly smaller** than the original cell in Panel 1, approximately half the area

- Each structure must have at least 30–40 px of clear landing zone for a callout marker
- Keep structures well-separated within each panel — do not crowd chromosomes
- Centrosomes should be clearly distinct from chromosomes
- The cleavage furrow in Telophase must be visibly different from the two separated cells in Cytokinesis
