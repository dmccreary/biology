# Image Generation Prompt — Mitochondria & Electron Transport Chain

## File
`mitochondria.png` — 1200×900 px, landscape 4:3

## Prompt

A detailed biological textbook illustration of a mitochondrion cross-section showing
both the overall organelle structure and a zoomed cross-section of the inner membrane
with the embedded protein complexes of the electron transport chain. Clean line art
with light color fills, white background.

**Overall mitochondrion structure (upper three-fifths of the image):**

Show the mitochondrion as a cut-away oval/bean shape revealing its internal structure:
- Outer membrane — smooth beige/tan outer boundary curve; freely permeable
- Inner membrane — highly folded dark brown inner membrane, following the outer membrane
  at the edges but projecting inward as finger-like or shelf-like folds called cristae
- Cristae — 4–6 visible folds of the inner membrane projecting inward into the matrix;
  they appear as parallel ridges or fingers of dark membrane, evenly spaced
- Intermembrane space — a visible pale blue aqueous gap between the outer and inner
  membranes; clearly visible at the top and sides of the organelle
- Matrix — the large pale amber/cream interior space enclosed by the inner membrane;
  dominant interior region; shows circular mtDNA as a small ring and ribosomes as dots

**Electron transport chain zoom (lower two-fifths of the image):**

A close-up cross-section of one crista (a single fold of the inner membrane) showing
both the matrix side (top of the zoom) and the intermembrane space side (bottom).
The inner membrane is a lipid bilayer strip running horizontally across the zoom.
The ETC protein complexes are embedded left to right in order of electron flow:

1. **Complex I (NADH dehydrogenase)** — the largest complex, deep blue L-shaped protein;
   one arm spans the membrane, the other arm protrudes into the matrix; NADH molecule
   shown as a yellow oval entering from the matrix side
2. **Complex II (succinate dehydrogenase)** — smaller teal-blue complex, slightly right
   of Complex I; FADH₂ molecule shown as a small orange oval entering from the matrix
3. **Coenzyme Q / ubiquinone (CoQ)** — a small orange mobile molecule shown within the
   membrane lipid bilayer between Complexes I/II and Complex III; curved arrow shows
   lateral diffusion
4. **Complex III (cytochrome bc1)** — medium purple complex in the center of the membrane
5. **Cytochrome c** — a small teal oval in the intermembrane space between Complex III
   and Complex IV; shown as a small globular protein on the IMS face
6. **Complex IV (cytochrome c oxidase)** — dark red complex right of center; an O₂
   molecule and H₂O molecule shown on the matrix face, indicating the final reduction
7. **ATP synthase (Complex V)** — rightmost complex; large pink mushroom shape with an
   Fₒ transmembrane rotor and a large F₁ catalytic head protruding into the matrix;
   a thick orange curved arrow from the IMS through the stalk shows H⁺ flow

Between and around complexes show:
- **H⁺ arrows** — upward red arrows from matrix into the IMS above Complexes I, III,
  and IV, representing proton pumping; and a downward orange arrow through ATP synthase
- **NADH and FADH₂ labels** — small coenzyme symbols in the matrix above Complex I and
  Complex II respectively (depicted as colored oval shapes only, no text)
- **O₂ → H₂O** — small molecular shapes on the matrix face of Complex IV

**No text of any kind in the image.** No labels, no chemical formula text, no arrows
with text, no callout lines, no annotation marks, no legend, no title, no figure caption
anywhere in the image.

Each structure must use a **distinct color** matching the descriptions above so that
callout markers can be visually matched to structures during calibration:
- Outer membrane: tan/beige
- Inner membrane & cristae: dark brown
- Intermembrane space: pale blue
- Matrix: pale amber/cream
- Complex I: deep blue
- Complex II: teal-blue
- Coenzyme Q: orange
- Complex III: purple
- Cytochrome c: teal/aqua
- Complex IV: dark red
- ATP synthase: pink/rose
- H⁺ arrows: red (pumping) and orange (through ATP synthase)
- NADH: yellow-gold; FADH₂: orange-yellow
- O₂/H₂O: sky blue and light blue

Leave enough clear space around each structure so that circular marker dots
(≈3–5% of image width) can be placed without overlapping neighbors.

Style: biological textbook illustration, clean line art with flat color fills,
white background. Suitable for AP Biology high school level.
Aspect ratio: landscape 4:3. Resolution: 1200×900 px.
