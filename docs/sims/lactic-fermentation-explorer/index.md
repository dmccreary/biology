## About This MicroSim

- Visualizes the lactic acid fermentation branch of cellular respiration with four sequential stages: glucose uptake, glycolysis, pyruvate accumulation, and lactate production.
- Highlights how NADH recycles to NAD⁺, allowing glycolysis to continue producing ATP when muscle tissue is in oxygen debt.
- Summarizes key outputs (lactate waste, net 2 ATP) and introduces representative fermenters such as muscle cells, red blood cells, and Lactobacillus.

## How to Use

1. Press **Start Simulation** to auto-step through each stage; use **Previous** and **Next** for manual control or pause with **Pause Simulation**.
2. Click any stage card to jump directly to that step for review.
3. Toggle **Show Oxygen Restored** to compare anaerobic vs reoxygenated conditions (mode strip updates the ETC status).
4. Adjust **Auto Step Delay** to set the autoplay pace.
5. Click an organism chip to pause the sim and read its lactic fermentation fun fact in the footer of the organism row.
