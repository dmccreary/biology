## About This MicroSim

This MicroSim demonstrates how the bent geometry and electronegativity difference of the water molecule produce a permanent dipole moment, and how this polarity leads to hydrogen bonding between adjacent water molecules.

## How to Use

Use the checkboxes to toggle layers on and off independently:

1. **Partial Charges** — shows the partial negative charge on oxygen and partial positive charges on hydrogen, with electronegativity values
2. **Dipole** — shows the net dipole arrow illustrating why the bent geometry prevents dipole cancellation
3. **H-Bonds** — shows hydrogen bonds to neighboring water molecules with bond length comparisons

Multiple layers can be active at the same time for comparison.
