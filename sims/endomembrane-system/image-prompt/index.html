    Image Prompt: Endomembrane System — Protein Secretion Pathway

    Critical Rules

    No text, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, leader lines, or annotation marks anywhere in the image.

    Image Specifications

    • Aspect ratio: landscape 4:3
    • Resolution: 1200 × 900 px
    • Style: biological textbook illustration, clean line art with light color fills, white background, AP Biology high school level
    • Background: clean white, no decorative elements

    Subject

    A schematic cross-section of a eukaryotic animal cell focused on the endomembrane system and the protein secretion pathway. The diagram should show the spatial relationships between the nucleus, rough ER, smooth ER, Golgi apparatus, transport vesicles, lysosomes, and plasma membrane. Small vesicles should be visible between compartments to suggest the transport pathway, but NO arrows or text.

    Structure Descriptions and Positions

    • Nucleus — Large dark blue oval with visible double-membrane nuclear envelope and nuclear pores; positioned in the upper-left quadrant of the cell; color: #2C3E80. Must have at least 50 px of clear, non-overlapping visual space.

    • Nuclear pores — Small gap-like openings in the nuclear envelope visible at several points around the nucleus; color: #1A5276. At least 3 pores visible as distinct breaks in the double membrane.

    • Rough endoplasmic reticulum — Network of blue-purple folded membrane cisternae studded with small dark dots (ribosomes) on the outer surface; extends from the nuclear envelope toward the center-right of the cell; color: #7E57C2. Must be visibly connected to the nuclear envelope. At least 40 px of clear space for a callout marker.

    • Smooth endoplasmic reticulum — Tubular membrane network continuous with rough ER but WITHOUT ribosome dots; positioned below/right of the rough ER; color: #9B59B6. Visually distinct from rough ER (no dots, more tubular).

    • COPII transport vesicle — Small round vesicle (circle) with a faint textured coat, positioned between the rough ER and the cis face of the Golgi; color: #48C9B0. At least 2 vesicles visible in this region.

    • Golgi apparatus (cis face) — The receiving side of the Golgi stack, facing the ER; left-most cisterna of the Golgi stack; color: #F7DC6F. The cis face should appear slightly convex toward the ER.

    • Golgi apparatus (medial cisternae) — The middle 2-3 cisternae of the Golgi stack; positioned in the center-right of the cell; color: #F5A623. Stacked flattened sacs, slightly curved.

    • Golgi apparatus (trans face) — The shipping side of the Golgi stack, facing the plasma membrane; right-most cisterna; color: #E67E22. Vesicles should appear to bud from this face.

    • Secretory vesicle — Medium-sized round vesicle budding from the trans Golgi and positioned between the Golgi and the plasma membrane; color: #2ECC71. At least 2 vesicles visible moving toward the membrane.

    • Lysosome — Small dark orange-brown sphere with visible granular content inside; positioned near the trans Golgi but separate from the secretory vesicles; color: #D35400. At least 1-2 visible.

    • Plasma membrane — Thin blue phospholipid bilayer forming the outer boundary of the cell along the bottom and right edges; color: #5DADE2. One area should show a vesicle fusing with the membrane (exocytosis), with the membrane slightly bulging outward.

    • Exocytosis site — A region of the plasma membrane where a secretory vesicle is fusing and releasing its contents to the extracellular space; color: #27AE60. The vesicle membrane should be merging with the plasma membrane.

    • Ribosomes (free) — Tiny dark gray dots scattered in the cytoplasm away from the ER; color: #555555. Clearly smaller and separate from the ribosomes on the rough ER.

    • Cytoplasm — Light cream/pale yellow background fill of the entire cell interior; color: #FFF8E7. Provides contrast for all organelles.

    Spatial Layout Requirements

    • The nucleus occupies the upper-left ~25% of the cell area
    • The rough ER extends from the nuclear envelope toward the center
    • The smooth ER is below/right of the rough ER
    • The Golgi apparatus is in the center-right, oriented with cis face toward the ER and trans face toward the plasma membrane
    • Transport vesicles are visible between ER → Golgi and Golgi → plasma membrane
    • The plasma membrane runs along the bottom and right edges
    • Lysosomes are near the trans Golgi but clearly separate
    • The overall flow suggests left-to-right movement: Nucleus → ER → Golgi → Plasma membrane

    What NOT to Include

    • No text, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, leader lines, or annotation marks anywhere in the image
    • No figure captions, scale bars, or legends
    • No gradient fills that blur structure boundaries
    • No artistic flourishes, bokeh, or photorealistic lighting
    • No mitochondria, cytoskeleton, or other organelles not in the endomembrane system

    REPEAT: No text, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, leader lines, or annotation marks anywhere in the image.
