    Signal Transduction Pathway — Image Generation Prompt

    No text, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, leader lines, or annotation marks anywhere in the image.

    Image Specifications

    • Aspect ratio: landscape 4:3
    • Resolution: 1200 × 900 px
    • Style: biological textbook illustration, clean line art with light color fills, white background, AP Biology high school level
    • Layout: Horizontal flow from left to right showing the three stages of a GPCR-mediated signal transduction pathway. A cell membrane runs horizontally across the upper third of the image, dividing extracellular space (top) from intracellular space (bottom two-thirds).

    Spatial Layout

    • Top region (above membrane): Extracellular space with a signal molecule (ligand) approaching from the upper-left
    • Membrane band: A phospholipid bilayer running horizontally across the image at approximately the upper quarter
    • Left third (at membrane): Reception stage — ligand binding to a GPCR (7-transmembrane receptor)
    • Center third (below membrane): Transduction stage — G-protein activation, adenylyl cyclase, cAMP production, PKA activation, phosphorylation cascade
    • Right third (below membrane): Response stage — activated enzymes, gene regulation in the nucleus (lower right)

    Structures and Colors

    No text of any kind in the image. No labels, numbers, or annotation marks.

    • Signal molecule (ligand) — small bright orange diamond shape; located in the extracellular space above the membrane, near the upper-left of the image, approaching the receptor. Color: #FF8C00. Must have at least 40 px of clear space.

    • GPCR (G-protein-coupled receptor) — a serpentine protein with 7 transmembrane segments shown as purple cylinders crossing the membrane; extracellular domain has a binding pocket shaped to receive the ligand; intracellular domain contacts the G-protein. Color: #8B5CF6. Located at left-center of the membrane. Must have at least 50 px of clear visual space.

    • Cell membrane (phospholipid bilayer) — horizontal double row of small circular heads (pale gold) with wavy tails, running the full width of the image at approximately the upper quarter. Color: #F5A623. Continuous band at least 60 px tall.

    • G-protein (inactive, GDP-bound) — a tri-lobed shape (alpha, beta, gamma subunits) sitting on the intracellular face of the membrane, directly below the GPCR. Alpha subunit is teal, beta and gamma are lighter teal. Color: #14B8A6. Located just below and to the right of the GPCR.

    • G-protein (active, GTP-bound alpha subunit) — the alpha subunit separated from beta-gamma, shown slightly to the right, glowing or highlighted to indicate activation. Color: #0D9488. Located to the right of the inactive G-protein, still near the membrane.

    • Adenylyl cyclase — a large enzyme embedded in the membrane, shown as a golden-yellow irregular oval shape with a channel/active site. Located in the center of the membrane, to the right of the G-protein. Color: #EAB308.

    • cAMP molecules — 4-5 small blue circular molecules clustered below the adenylyl cyclase, indicating second messenger production. Color: #3B82F6. Located in the center of the intracellular space, below adenylyl cyclase.

    • PKA (protein kinase A) — a medium green diamond-shaped enzyme, shown in the center-lower area of the image, below the cAMP molecules. Color: #22C55E.

    • Phosphorylation cascade — 3 stacked oval shapes in progressively darker shades of green, each with a small pink phosphate circle attached, arranged vertically below PKA. Colors: #4ADE80 (top), #16A34A (middle), #15803D (bottom). Located in the center-right area.

    • Target enzyme (activated) — a large red-orange rectangular shape at the right side of the image, representing the final activated enzyme that produces the cellular response. Color: #EF4444. Located right-center.

    • Nucleus — a large circular structure with a double-membrane envelope (dark gray outline) in the lower-right corner of the image. Interior is pale lavender. Color: #A78BFA (envelope), #EDE9FE (interior). Contains a visible nucleolus.

    • Transcription factors — small magenta oval shapes entering the nucleus through a nuclear pore. Color: #EC4899. Located at the nuclear envelope boundary.

    • Gene expression response — inside the nucleus, a short segment of DNA double helix (blue and red strands) with a wavy RNA strand emerging. Color: #6366F1 (DNA), #F43F5E (RNA).

    Structure Connections (visual flow)

    • The ligand should appear to be fitting into the GPCR binding pocket
    • The G-protein should be visually adjacent to the GPCR intracellular domain
    • The active G-alpha should be visually approaching the adenylyl cyclase
    • cAMP molecules should appear to be emanating from the adenylyl cyclase
    • A visual flow should be apparent from cAMP → PKA → cascade → responses
    • Transcription factors should appear to be moving toward the nucleus

    Key Requirements

    • All structures must be clearly distinct with enough space for a 30-40 px callout marker
    • Colors must be visually distinguishable from each other
    • The overall layout should read left-to-right as: Reception → Transduction → Response
    • The membrane should clearly separate extracellular from intracellular space
