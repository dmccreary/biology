    Meiosis Stages Explorer — Image Generation Prompt

    No text, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, leader lines, or annotation marks anywhere in the image.

    Overview

    Create a biological textbook illustration showing all stages of meiosis arranged in two horizontal rows. The top row shows Meiosis I (5 stages) and the bottom row shows Meiosis II (4 stages). A thin horizontal light gray divider line separates the two rows.

    Technical Specifications

    • Aspect ratio: landscape 4:3
    • Resolution: 1200 × 900 px
    • Style: biological textbook illustration, clean line art with light color fills, white background, AP Biology high school level
    • No text, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, leader lines, or annotation marks anywhere in the image.

    Layout

    • Top row (~48% of image height): Five cells arranged left to right showing Meiosis I stages: Interphase → Prophase I → Metaphase I → Anaphase I → Telophase I/Cytokinesis I
    • Bottom row (~48% of image height): Four cells arranged left to right showing Meiosis II stages: Prophase II → Metaphase II → Anaphase II → Telophase II/Cytokinesis II
    • Thin vertical light gray divider lines separate each stage within a row
    • Thin horizontal light gray divider line between the two rows
    • Each cell is drawn as a large rounded shape centered in its panel

    Chromosome Convention

    Use 2n = 4 (two homologous pairs). Color-code by parental origin: - Maternal chromosomes: Pink/red shades (#D94A4A for one pair, #E57373 for the other) - Paternal chromosomes: Blue shades (#4A90D9 for one pair, #64B5F6 for the other) - After crossing over in Prophase I, recombinant segments show striped pink-blue pattern

    Stage-by-Stage Structure Details

    TOP ROW — Meiosis I

    Panel 1 — Interphase (leftmost ~20%)

    • Cell outline: large circle, light gray border, pale cream fill
    • Chromosomes: 4 thin, diffuse chromatin threads (2 pink/red, 2 blue) — NOT condensed, just loose wavy lines spread throughout the nucleus
    • Nuclear envelope: complete dark gray circle
    • Nucleolus: small dark purple circle inside
    • Centrosomes: single orange dot near the nuclear envelope (not yet duplicated visually)

    Panel 2 — Prophase I (~20%)

    • Cell outline: large circle, light gray border, pale cream fill
    • Chromosomes: 4 condensed X-shaped sister chromatids, paired into 2 tetrads (bivalents). Each tetrad shows one pink/red chromosome tightly paired alongside one blue chromosome. At the contact point between homologs, show a small chiasma (X-shaped crossover point) with color mixing
    • Centrosomes: two orange circles migrating to opposite sides
    • Spindle fibers: thin green lines beginning to radiate from centrosomes
    • Nuclear envelope: dark gray dashed circle, beginning to fragment (gaps visible)

    Panel 3 — Metaphase I (center ~20%)

    • Cell outline: large circle, light gray border, pale cream fill
    • Chromosomes: 2 tetrads (bivalents) aligned at the metaphase plate (vertical center line). Each tetrad shows homologous pair side by side — one pink/red, one blue. Chiasmata still visible
    • Spindle fibers: green lines from orange centrosomes at left and right poles, attached to the kinetochores of homologous chromosomes (NOT individual chromatids)
    • Metaphase plate: subtle dashed magenta vertical line at center
    • No nuclear envelope

    Panel 4 — Anaphase I (~20%)

    • Cell outline: slightly elongated oval, light gray border, pale cream fill
    • Chromosomes: Homologous pairs separating — 2 pink/red X-shaped chromosomes moving left, 2 blue X-shaped chromosomes moving right. Sister chromatids are STILL joined (each chromosome is still X-shaped, not V-shaped). Some chromosomes show recombinant segments (mixed pink-blue coloring from crossing over)
    • Spindle fibers: green lines pulling homologs to opposite poles
    • Cell is visibly stretching

    Panel 5 — Telophase I / Cytokinesis I (rightmost ~20%)

    • Two cells pinching apart with a cleavage furrow between them
    • Left cell: contains 2 pink/red X-shaped chromosomes (still as sister chromatids joined at centromere), with some recombinant blue segments
    • Right cell: contains 2 blue X-shaped chromosomes (still as sister chromatids), with some recombinant pink segments
    • Nuclear envelopes: dark gray circles beginning to reform around each chromosome set
    • Cleavage furrow: pink contractile ring pinching at the center

    BOTTOM ROW — Meiosis II

    Panel 6 — Prophase II (leftmost ~25%)

    • Two smaller cells side by side (products of Meiosis I)
    • Left cell: 2 condensed X-shaped chromosomes (pink/red, some with recombinant segments), orange centrosomes at poles, thin green spindle fibers forming, nuclear envelope fragmenting (dashed)
    • Right cell: 2 condensed X-shaped chromosomes (blue, some with recombinant segments), same spindle formation

    Panel 7 — Metaphase II (~25%)

    • Two smaller cells side by side
    • Left cell: 2 X-shaped chromosomes aligned at metaphase plate (vertical center), green spindle fibers from orange centrosomes attached to individual kinetochores of sister chromatids
    • Right cell: same arrangement with blue chromosomes
    • Dashed magenta metaphase plate line visible in each cell

    Panel 8 — Anaphase II (~25%)

    • Two smaller cells side by side, each slightly elongated
    • Left cell: 4 individual V-shaped chromatids (no longer X-shaped) — 2 moving left, 2 moving right. Pink/red with some recombinant blue segments. Green spindle fibers pulling them apart
    • Right cell: same with blue chromatids, some recombinant pink segments

    Panel 9 — Telophase II / Cytokinesis II (rightmost ~25%)

    • Four small haploid cells arranged in a 2×2 cluster
    • Each cell contains 2 single chromatids (not X-shaped anymore — just simple rod shapes) representing n = 2
    • Due to crossing over, each cell has a unique combination of maternal and paternal segments — no two cells are identical in their color patterns
    • Nuclear envelopes: complete dark gray circles in each cell
    • Cleavage furrows: pink pinch marks between dividing cells

    Color Key (hex values)

    Maternal chromosomes — pair 2 Light red #E57373
    Paternal chromosomes — pair 1 Blue #4A90D9

    Spindle fibers / microtubules Green #4CAF50
    Nuclear envelope Dark gray #555555
    Centrosomes Orange #FF9800
    Metaphase plate line Magenta dashed #E91E9F

    Nucleolus Dark purple #6A1B9A

    Cell fill Pale cream #FFF8E1

    Critical Reminders

    • Each structure must have at least 30–40 px of clear landing zone for a callout marker
    • Chromosomes in Anaphase I are STILL X-shaped (sister chromatids joined) — only in Anaphase II do they separate into V-shaped individual chromatids
    • The four final cells must each be visually UNIQUE in chromosome coloring to show genetic diversity from crossing over and independent assortment
    • Keep structures well-separated within each panel
    • Bottom row cells should be noticeably smaller than top row cells
